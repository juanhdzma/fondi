# Design

## Direction

Fondi is a private wealth console: quiet, precise, and familiar. It borrows Apple's hierarchy, materials, typography, and interaction discipline without copying product chrome.

## Composition

- Desktop uses a translucent sidebar and a wide content canvas.
- Mobile uses a compact top bar and a persistent bottom tab bar.
- The summary prioritizes one large chart, a compact metric rail, and a unified participant list.
- Administration uses a responsive workspace grid with the movement form as the primary task.

## Visual System

- System typography with optical sizing, tight display tracking, and tabular financial figures.
- Cool grouped background, white functional surfaces, deep navy brand accent, and restrained semantic green and red.
- Depth comes from one soft offset shadow per floating surface. Dividers organize dense lists; cards do not nest.
- Radii stay between 12px and 16px. Pills are reserved for compact statuses and segmented controls.

## Interaction

- Frequent navigation changes state immediately.
- Buttons provide 120ms press feedback; hover effects are gated to fine pointers.
- Toasts use an interruptible 200ms transform and opacity transition.
- Reduced motion removes transforms, reduced transparency replaces blur, and increased contrast strengthens boundaries.

## Content

Use direct Spanish labels. Values keep their currency visible, gains use semantic color, and destructive or irreversible actions keep explicit warnings.
